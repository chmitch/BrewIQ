# Recipe library (extract kit recipes)

## Oatmeal Stout
Style: Oatmeal Stout
Source: Northern Brewer
Batch size: 5 gal
OG: 2.050
ABV: 4.8 %
IBU: 25
Yeast (library code): S-04
Yeast notes: Kit suggests Safale S-04 (64-75 F), Imperial A01 House (62-70 F), Omega OYL-006 British Ale I (64-72 F) or Wyeast 1098 (64-75 F).
Timeline: 2 weeks primary, 2 weeks secondary, 2 weeks in the bottle (about 6 weeks).
Ingredients:
- fermentable: Flaked oats, 1 lb (steep)
- fermentable: English dark crystal malt, 0.5 lb (steep)
- fermentable: Chocolate malt, 0.5 lb (steep)
- fermentable: Roasted barley, 0.5 lb (steep)
- fermentable: Gold liquid malt extract, 6 lb (60 min)
- fermentable: Dark dry malt extract, 1 lb (60 min)
- hop: Glacier, 1 oz (60 min)
